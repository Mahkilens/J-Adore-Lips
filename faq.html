Frequently Asked

Common Questions

How do I use the lip plumper?

Apply directly to clean lips. Wear alone for a glossy look or layer over lip liner or lipstick for extra shine.

Will it feel tingly?

Some plumpers can create a light tingling sensation. If you have sensitive skin, patch test first and discontinue use if irritation occurs.

How long does shipping take?

Shipping times depend on your location and the method selected at checkout. You’ll receive tracking details once your order ships.

Do you accept returns?

If there’s an issue with your order, contact us and we’ll help you out. For hygiene reasons, opened cosmetics may have limitations—reach out and we’ll confirm your options.

How can I contact support?

Email us at jadorelips90@gmail.com or use the contact form.
